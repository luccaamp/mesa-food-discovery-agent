Derived serving artifacts for the independent Mesa / Meal Discovery AI project.

Source: Delivery Hero Recommendation Dataset (DHRD), Singapore official catalog
and training orders. Authors: Yernat Assylbekov, Raghav Bali, Luke Bovard,
Christian Klaue. RecSys 2023, DOI 10.1145/3604915.3610242.

The publisher states that the DHRD dataset is released under the MIT license:
https://github.com/deliveryhero/dh-reco-dataset#license
https://opensource.org/license/mit/

These processed artifacts contain product/vendor identifiers, original product
names, normalized prices, cuisine labels, popularity counts and aggregated
co-basket neighbor scores. They do not contain individual customer or order
tables. Recommendations are fitted only through relative day 61. Official test
orders are not used. No live availability or ingredient/allergen guarantees.
